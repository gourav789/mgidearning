Because the file is delivered electronically and its contents are accessible immediately on delivery, all sales are final and no refunds are provided once the purchase is completed. By buying, you acknowledge and agree that you are purchasing instant-access digital content and that you waive any right to a refund to the extent permitted by applicable law.

2. Payment failures and wrong/duplicate charges

Payments are processed by Razorpay. If a payment fails but money is debited from your account, or if you are charged more than once or in error, that is a payment-processing matter handled by Razorpay:

  • Failed payments where money was debited are normally auto-reversed by your bank / Razorpay within the standard processing time (often up to 5–7 business days).

These payment-failure reversals are not “refunds of the product” — they are corrections of a payment that did not result in a valid purchase.

This is a one-time digital purchase (not a subscription), so there is nothing to cancel after delivery. If you close the checkout before paying, no charge is made.
